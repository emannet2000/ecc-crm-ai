    Security & compliance

    Built to hold passports

    Immigration records are among the most sensitive documents a person will ever share. The system is built around that assumption, across every region.

    • Encrypted at rest and in transit

      Documents sit on encrypted storage and travel over TLS. Nothing leaves the system unencrypted, in any region.

    • Role-based access

      Advisors see only their own cases. Regional managers see their region. Admins see the whole system — and every one of those actions is logged.

    • Full audit trail

      Every view, edit, download and status change is written to an append-only log. Filter by user, date, region or case reference.

    • Multi-region data residency

      Data can be pinned to a specific region (EU, APAC, MENA) to satisfy local regulatory requirements. Admins choose the residency policy per workspace.

    Data residency 3 regions EU · APAC · MENA
    Retention 5 years then automatic deletion
    Audit log Append-only every action recorded
    Compliance GDPR + PDPA-aligned
    Onboarding

    From spreadsheet to system in a day

    Most regional offices are fully moved over inside a single working day. No imports are lost, no cases go dark.

    • 1

      Import your existing records

      Upload your current contacts, leads and cases from CSV or an existing spreadsheet. We map the columns and verify the import before switching over.

    • 2

      Set up regions and roles

      Create users, assign roles (advisor, regional manager, admin) and link them to their region. Everyone signs in with their work email.

    • 3

      Configure document templates

      Load the standard document checklists for each destination and service. New cases start with the right requirements already listed.

    • 4

      Start tracking cases

      Move your open cases into the pipeline, assign them to advisors, and open the daily reports. From that point it runs itself.
